About

Arun Prakash Baag

Engineering team lead in automotive CAE and simulation, certified SAFe POPM and Google PM.

I lead a team of seven to eight CAE engineers at Jaguar Land Rover, delivering closures simulation across most of the Land Rover range and owning the work from planning through to delivery. My own technical background is in pedestrian safety CAE, structural closures analysis and warranty root-cause simulation, using LS-DYNA, ABAQUS and OptiStruct.

Highlights include pedestrian head impact simulation on two mainstream programmes, covering passive and active bonnets with and without airbags; working with ARUP on a head impact result mapping tool in PRIMER; and building an application that automates ECE127 and EuNCAP impact-point set-up, now part of the standard CAE workflow at JLR.

I hold a Six Sigma Green Belt and a Level 4 Data Analyst qualification with Distinction, and have taken my CAE work into data analysis and machine learning to bring data into simulation-heavy engineering decisions. The Google Project Management Certificate and the AI-Empowered SAFe Product Owner/Product Manager certification sit naturally alongside this: much of CAE delivery comes down to sequencing, risk and getting the right conversation to happen at the right time.

Developing engineers has run through my whole career. I started out training new engineers on CAD and CAE software, and today I lead and mentor a CAE team, run an 8-week CAE training programme and give webinars for engineers moving into simulation.
