CAE Lab Training

From statics to client-ready simulation in eight weeks

A structured, 40-session programme for engineering graduates and engineers moving into CAE. It covers the theory, the tools, and how real CAE work is scoped, delivered and reported.

WEEKS 1–2FoundationsMechanics, materials, CAD and the maths behind FEM.
WEEKS 3–5Structural CAEMeshing, linear, nonlinear, fatigue, crash and optimisation.
WEEK 6CFD & thermalFluent, OpenFOAM and thermal-structural coupling.
WEEKS 7–8Professional practiceQuality, reports, projects, RFQs, proposals and a capstone.
Curriculum

Week by week

Each day has slides, a hands-on exercise and a short quiz. Every Friday ends with an assessment, and the programme closes with a full-cycle capstone project.

WEEK 1

Engineering foundations

  1. Welcome to the CAE Lab and the CAE industry
  2. Engineering mechanics: statics
  3. Strength of materials
  4. Materials and failure theories
  5. Engineering drawings, GD&T and Week 1 test
WEEK 2

CAD, maths and FEM theory

  1. CAD modelling for CAE
  2. Geometry clean-up and mid-surfacing
  3. Mathematics for FEA
  4. FEM theory I: springs, bars and assembly
  5. FEM theory II: elements and Week 2 test
WEEK 3

Pre-processing and meshing

  1. HyperMesh interface and workflow
  2. 2D shell meshing and mesh quality
  3. 3D solid meshing: tet and hex
  4. Materials, properties and connections
  5. Loads, boundary conditions and mini-project
WEEK 4

Linear structural analysis

  1. Linear static analysis
  2. Results, convergence and singularities
  3. Modal analysis
  4. Buckling and dynamic response
  5. Fatigue analysis and Week 4 test
WEEK 5

Nonlinear, dynamics, crash and optimisation

  1. Material nonlinearity: plasticity
  2. Contact nonlinearity and bolt pretension
  3. Geometric nonlinearity and solution control
  4. Explicit dynamics: drop test and crash
  5. Topology optimisation, open-source FEA and Week 5 test
WEEK 6

CFD and thermal

  1. Fluid mechanics fundamentals
  2. CFD theory: meshing and turbulence
  3. CFD workflow in ANSYS Fluent
  4. OpenFOAM: open-source CFD
  5. Heat transfer, thermal-structural and Week 6 test
WEEK 7

Running a CAE lab

  1. Infrastructure, licences, HPC and data
  2. Quality assurance, verification and validation
  3. Technical report writing
  4. Project management for CAE
  5. Confidentiality, IP and capstone kick-off
WEEK 8

Working with clients

  1. Understanding RFQs and scoping
  2. Estimation, pricing and proposals
  3. Client communication and pitching
  4. Invoicing, contracts and payments
  5. Capstone presentations and graduation
Who it's for
  • Mechanical and automotive engineering graduates who want a first simulation role
  • Design engineers who want to run and judge their own analyses
  • Teams setting up a new CAE lab who need shared standards from day one
