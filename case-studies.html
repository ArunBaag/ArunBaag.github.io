Case studies

Programme work and tools, in brief

Selected work from fifteen years as a CAE Lead at Jaguar Land Rover. Details are kept at the level that is public.

Closures and PHI · CAE Lead

Range Rover & Range Rover Sport

Challenge

Two flagship programmes needed closures durability, NVH and pedestrian head impact delivered at the same time, with the resources of one team.

What I did

Ran both programmes in parallel. Introduced the strap design concept on the doors, new processes for single-bolt hinges and bolt pretension, and dynamic simulation for power doors. Delivered head impact analysis on both passive bonnets.

Outcome

Both programmes delivered by one team, with significant door weight savings and new simulation methods that the team still uses.

Closures, PHI and Agile delivery · CAE Lead

Defender & EV Defender

Challenge

Early Defender work needed a taildoor that could carry a 40 kg spare tyre. On the EV Defender, a new passive bonnet had to be delivered inside an Agile squad.

What I did

Led the pre-PS work on doors, lids and trims and supported the taildoor concept. On EV Defender, lead bonnet and fender durability, NVH and head impact, planning CAE work in sprints and prioritising it by business value.

Outcome

A taildoor concept that carries a 40 kg tyre and set the path for later optimisation; CAE delivered sprint by sprint on a new bonnet feature.

Closures and active bonnet PHI · CAE Lead

Range Rover Evoque

Challenge

Joined midway through the programme, with active bonnet head impact to deliver and a persistent tailgate boom to resolve.

What I did

Took over closures (doors, lids, trims) and head impact CAE, learned and delivered active bonnet PHI for the first time, and brought in noise transfer function analysis for the tailgate.

Outcome

Active bonnet head impact delivered and the tailgate boom traced and resolved through NTF analysis.

Closures durability and NVH · CAE Lead

Discovery

Challenge

The programme had the heaviest rear tailgate in the range and demanding durability and NVH targets for doors and lids.

What I did

Led closures durability and NVH, introduced a laser blank concept into the front door system, and worked the tailgate optimisation.

Outcome

Laser blank front door adopted and the programme's hardest closures problem, the heavy tailgate, optimised.

Python · JLR Pedestrian Safety CAE

PHI automation tool

Challenge

Setting up ECE127 and EuNCAP impact points by hand was slow and easy to get wrong on every new bonnet.

What I did

Wrote a Python application that sets up the impact points automatically, then extended it into a suite covering pre-processing, post-processing and robustness. Worked with ARUP on a results mapping tool in PRIMER.

Outcome

Now part of the standard JLR head impact CAE workflow, giving analysts consistent set-ups in a fraction of the time.

Tableau · JLR Quality and CAE

Closures quality dashboard

Challenge

Closures quality issues across programmes sat in spreadsheets, making trends hard to see and act on.

What I did

Built an interactive data visualisation tool with JLR quality engineers, plus Tableau dashboards that present CAE results to engineers and managers.

Outcome

Quality issues tracked across programmes in one place, and CAE results available to stakeholders without waiting for a report.
